$$
[x^n]x^kA(x)=a_{n-k},\quad [x^n]A(cx)=c^na_n,\quad [x^n]A'(x)=(n+1)a_{n+1},
$$


$$
xA'(x)=\sum_{n\ge0}na_nx^n,\quad\int_0^xA(t)\,dt=\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{a_n}{n+1}x^{n+1}.
$$


标记次数 $u$：若 $A(x,u)=\sum_{n,k}a_{n,k}x^nu^k$，则


$$
[x^n]\left.\frac{\partial^r}{\partial u^r}A(x,u)\right|_{u=1}=\sum_k k^{\underline r}a_{n,k}.
$$
