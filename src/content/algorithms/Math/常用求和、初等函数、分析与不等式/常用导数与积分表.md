$$
\frac{d}{dx}x^a=ax^{a-1},\quad\frac{d}{dx}\ln|x|=\frac1x,\quad\frac{d}{dx}e^{ax}=ae^{ax},\quad\frac{d}{dx}a^x=a^x\ln a\ (a>0),
$$


$$
\frac{d}{dx}\sin x=\cos x,\quad\frac{d}{dx}\cos x=-\sin x,\quad\frac{d}{dx}\tan x=\sec^2x,\quad\frac{d}{dx}\arctan x=\frac1{1+x^2}.
$$



$$
\int x^a\,dx=\frac{x^{a+1}}{a+1}+C\quad(a\ne-1),\quad\int\frac{dx}{x}=\ln|x|+C,
$$


$$
\int e^{ax}\,dx=\frac{e^{ax}}a+C\quad(a\ne0),\quad\int\sin(ax)\,dx=-\frac{\cos(ax)}a+C,\quad\int\cos(ax)\,dx=\frac{\sin(ax)}a+C,
$$


$$
\int\frac{dx}{a^2+x^2}=\frac1a\arctan\frac xa+C\quad(a>0),\quad\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}=\arcsin\frac xa+C\quad(|x|<a,a>0),
$$


$$
\int\frac{dx}{x^2-a^2}=\frac1{2a}\ln\left|\frac{x-a}{x+a}\right|+C\quad(a\ne0),
$$


$$
\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}=\ln\left|x+\sqrt{x^2+a^2}\right|+C\quad(a>0).
$$


分部积分、变量代换：


$$
\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du,\qquad\int f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int f(t)\,dt\quad(t=g(x)).
$$
